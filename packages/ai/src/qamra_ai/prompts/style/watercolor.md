## Look
Premium watercolor and gouache children's picture-book painting on white textured paper: clean, bright,
transparent washes with clear, rich color and real contrast, deep indigo and violet shadows, and luminous
highlights. The light is the scene's own (soft daylight, a golden evening or moonlight), and under it skin,
hair and clothes keep their true, clear colors. Soft wet edges and visible paper grain, with crisp detail on
faces and hands; atmospheric depth with soft foreground framing elements (leaves, arches, lanterns). Never
flat or pale. Each child keeps their own face shape and proportions, eye size, hair and real age: the
softness is in the paint, never a younger or rounder face.

## Character
Paint the child in clean, bright, transparent watercolor washes, in even, neutral daylight: skin, hair,
eyes and clothes in their true, clear colors, with crisp detail on the face.

## Negative
- No photorealism, no 3D-render look, no harsh black outlines, no neon colors, no anime.
- No flat, pale or washed-out backgrounds.
- No baby face, no generic round-faced child, no monochrome or sepia sketch.
- No sepia, brown, amber or yellow cast over the whole picture, no muddy browns or grays.
