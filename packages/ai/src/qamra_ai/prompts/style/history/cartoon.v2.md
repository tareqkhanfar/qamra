## Look
Bright, clean 2D cartoon picture-book illustration: bold flat color blocks with soft two-tone cel shading,
smooth confident outlines drawn in a darker shade of each fill color, and crisp print-ready edges. A cheerful
saturated palette of sky blue, sunny yellow, grass green, coral and warm cream, bright but never neon. Simple
rounded shapes, big friendly eyes, clear silhouettes, and backgrounds made of a few large simple shapes.
Children keep their real face shape, skin tone and hairstyle, with slightly large heads.

## Negative
- No photorealism, no 3D rendering, no watercolor textures or paper grain, no pure-black heavy outlines.
- No neon colors, no busy patterns, no anime.
