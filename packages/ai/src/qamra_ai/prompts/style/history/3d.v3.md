## Look
A polished 3D animated-film look: appealing stylized rendering with soft subsurface-scattering skin, cinematic
warm key light with a gentle rim light and soft volumetric glow, soft global illumination, and a shallow depth
of field. Rich, saturated, warm colors; clean stylized materials (soft fabric, matte painted wood, smooth
limestone); a few floating golden light particles in magical moments. The stylization is in the materials,
light and simplified detail, never in the child's face: each child keeps their own face shape and proportions,
eye size, nose, smile, hair, skin tone and real age, clearly recognizable. Stylized, never realistic.

## Negative
- No photorealism, no realistic skin pores, no uncanny realism, no glossy plastic-doll or vinyl-toy look.
- No oversized eyes or heads, no baby face, no generic animated-film child face.
- No dark, gritty or horror lighting. No existing film, studio, game or toy characters or their look-alikes.
