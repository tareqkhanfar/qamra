## Look
A warm, family-friendly 3D animated-film look: soft rounded characters with stylized proportions (slightly
large heads, big expressive eyes), gentle soft-glow skin, soft global illumination and warm key light, a
shallow depth of field, and clean stylized materials (soft fabric, matte painted wood, smooth stone). Colors
are rich and warm but gentle. Children keep their real face shape, skin tone and hairstyle, clearly
recognizable, never realistic.

## Negative
- No photorealism, no realistic skin pores, no uncanny realism, no glossy plastic-doll look.
- No dark, gritty or horror lighting. No existing film, game or toy characters.
