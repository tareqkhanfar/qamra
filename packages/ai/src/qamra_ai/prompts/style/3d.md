## Look
A polished 3D animated-film look: a stylized CG rendering with subsurface-scattering skin, cinematic warm key
light with a gentle rim light and soft volumetric glow, soft global illumination, and a shallow depth of field.
Rich, saturated, warm colors; clean stylized materials (soft fabric, matte painted wood, smooth limestone); a
few floating golden light particles in magical moments. Each child is a stylized 3D likeness of a real child,
as if the head were sculpted from the photo: the real head shape and face proportions, the jaw, chin and
cheeks as they are, and the eyes at the photo's own size and shape (narrow eyes stay narrow), with
simplified, smooth surfaces. The stylization is in the materials, light and simplified detail, never in the
child's face: each child keeps their own face shape and proportions, eye size, nose, smile, hair and its true
color, skin tone and real age, clearly recognizable. Stylized, never realistic.

## Negative
- No photorealism, no realistic skin pores, no uncanny realism, no glossy plastic-doll or vinyl-toy look.
- No oversized eyes or heads, no big round animated-film eyes, no round, wide or chubby face, no puffy baby
  cheeks, no button nose, no short chin, no baby face, no generic animated-film child face.
- No dark, gritty or horror lighting. No existing film, studio, game or toy characters or their look-alikes.
