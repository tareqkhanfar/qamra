## Look
Bright, clean 2D cartoon picture-book illustration: bold flat color blocks with crisp two-tone cel shading,
smooth confident outlines drawn in a darker shade of each fill color, and crisp print-ready edges. A cheerful
saturated palette of sky blue, sunny yellow, grass green, coral and warm cream, bright but never neon. Clear
silhouettes, and backgrounds made of a few large simple shapes. People are drawn like a cartoonist's portrait
of a real child: the outline of the head, face, jaw and chin and the places of the features follow the
reference, simplified in line and color but never reshaped. Each child keeps their own face shape and
proportions, eye size, nose, smile, skin tone, hairstyle and real age, clearly recognizable.

## Negative
- No photorealism, no 3D rendering, no watercolor textures or paper grain, no pure-black heavy outlines.
- No oversized eyes or heads, no round, wide or chubby cartoon face, no button nose, no short chin, no baby
  face, no generic cartoon child.
- No neon colors, no busy patterns, no anime.
