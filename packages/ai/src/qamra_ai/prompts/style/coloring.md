## Look
Black-and-white line art for a children's coloring book: clean, closed black outlines of an even, medium-bold
width on a pure white background, with large simple areas that small hands can color. No fills: every area
stays white. Faces are drawn simply, with clear eyes, nose and smile, and the hero keeps a recognizable
hairstyle or hijab shape. Backgrounds are simple and uncluttered.

## Negative
- No color, no gray tones, no shading, no hatching, no gradients, no textures.
- No thin, sketchy or broken lines, and no tiny details that are hard to color.
