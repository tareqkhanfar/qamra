portrait: natural proportions, natural eye size, real tones in the face, hair in strands, real fabric, soft
painterly light, still a painting and never a photograph. Three sections apply to this style only:
- `House style` replaces the house style's Style paragraph (its "picture-book illustration", "rounded",
  "slightly large heads" and "not photorealistic" pull any painting back to a cartoon);
- `House negative` replaces the house's Negative list: the same lines without "No photorealism" (this
  style's own Negative says "no photograph or camera look", which keeps a painting without pushing it back
  to a cartoon);
- `Sheet` is added to the character-sheet prompt: a clothed three-view sheet that cuts out cleanly.
Words about children's skin, pores, bodies or "glamorous" children, and life-drawing words ("figure study",
"painted from life", "anatomy"), can make an image model refuse a child: the face is described by its
complexion and tones only. v2 is kept in `history/semi-realistic.v2.md`.

## Look
Realistic painting in the manner of a fine-art oil portrait, carried through the whole scene and whatever pose
and action it describes. Natural, true-to-age proportions: a young child's real head size for their height,
natural-size eyes with real irises, lids and lashes, and a natural nose, lips and ears. The face is modeled
with real tonal variation in the child's own complexion: warmer cheeks, nose and ears, cooler half-tones at the
temples and jaw, and a soft, warm glow where the light falls. Hair is real hair, with its true color, texture,
hairline and parting, painted in locks with fine strands catching the light. Clothes have real fabric weight
and natural folds. Soft, painterly light: one warm key light, a gentle fill and a quiet rim light, with soft
natural shadows that model every form. Faces and hands are softly blended, with gentle brushwork still
visible; clothes and backgrounds show confident, visible brushwork, and backgrounds have real depth and
atmosphere, a little softer than the figures. Likeness comes first: the face shape, eyes, eyebrows, nose,
mouth, complexion, hairline and hairstyle stay so close to the reference that a parent knows their own child
at a glance. Warm, gentle and dignified. Unmistakably a hand-made painting, never a photograph.

## Negative
- No cartoon, comic, anime or picture-book stylization: no enlarged heads or eyes, no tiny button noses, no
  faces reduced to simple circles, no drawn or ink outlines.
- No flat or cel shading, no watercolor washes or paper grain, no 3D-render, clay, plastic or doll look.
- No photograph or camera look: no lens blur or bokeh, no film grain, no HDR; the brushwork stays visible.
- No uncanny or waxy faces, no glassy eyes; children look exactly their real age.
- No clumsy hands: hands are painted carefully, with five fingers on each visible hand.

## House style
Paintings for a premium hardcover book for young children. The medium and rendering come from the painting
medium below; everything else here is shared by every picture of the book.
The palette is warm moonlit-and-golden: deep indigo and night blue, moon gold and honey, and creamy whites,
with gentle sage-green and coral accents. The palette lives in the light, the sky, the walls and the fabrics;
faces, hair and eyes keep their own true colors. Light is warm with a golden glow, and every scene has depth:
foreground, midground and background. Compositions are clear and backgrounds uncluttered, while every form
keeps its natural, realistic detail.
People are painted as real people, with natural, true-to-age proportions and warm, natural, gentle
expressions. Not a cartoon, not anime, not a 3D render and not a photograph.

## House negative
- No text of any kind: no letters, words, numbers, signs with writing, book titles, labels, speech bubbles,
  logos, brand names or watermarks. All text is added later by the book designer.
- No page numbers, captions, signatures, stamps or marks in the corners: the printed book adds its own page numbers.
- Books, boxes, shelves and walls stay plain: book spines and covers are blocks of color, with no titles;
  no alphabet posters, charts, calendars, name labels or price tags.
- No neon colors.
- No extra or missing fingers, no extra limbs, no distorted, melted or duplicated faces.
- No existing cartoon, film or game characters.

## Sheet
Paint a full-length character sheet with three views of the child, fully clothed in the reference outfit, in
this realistic painted style: not a cartoon model sheet. If a reference is a drawing or a painting rather than
a photo, take only the child's face, hair and complexion from it, never its drawing style, and paint the child
with natural, true-to-life features and proportions. These sheet rules come before any light, glow, depth or
scenery wording above: light the sheet with even, soft light and no rim light or glow at the edges. Each
figure stands against the plain background with no floor or scenery and at most a faint, soft shadow under
the feet. Its outer edge is painted crisp and clean against the background (hair as defined locks, never
fading into the background), with clear empty background between the three figures, so each one cuts out
cleanly for the activity books.
