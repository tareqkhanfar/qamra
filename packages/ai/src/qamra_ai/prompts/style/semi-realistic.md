## Look
A semi-realistic painted storybook portrait style: soft digital painting with natural proportions and gentle
visible brushwork, warm natural light, and softened, idealized features that stay clearly recognizable. It
reads as a classic painted illustration, never a photograph: simplified skin, soft edges, painterly
backgrounds with gentle depth.

## Negative
- No photorealism, no photographic textures, no skin pores, no lens effects, no uncanny realism.
- Children must stay clearly illustrated and age-appropriate: no adult-looking or glamorized children.
