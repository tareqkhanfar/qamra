## 1. What to build

1. **Routes:**
   - `/shop`
   - `/quiz`
   - `/stories/[slug]` (StoryProduct)
   - `/workbooks/[product]` (dawseyeh, journey, family: the same template, options from product data)
   - `/free-cover/result`
   - `/create/.../addons`
   - `/cart`
   
   Match the design: tokens, sticky bottom bar, 44 px+ targets, RTL.
2. **Everything is data from admin, nothing hard-coded:**
   - product lines, variants, prices, add-ons (with dependencies), art-style availability per line (Classic shows 3D / semi-realistic as "متوفر في سحري" until templates exist), bundle discount rule, quiz rules.
   - The prices in the design are the suggested defaults from Addendum 4/5/6/7. Seed them, but read from the DB.
3. **Quiz logic:** store it as editable rules (age × goal × holds-pen → product + alternative). Default rules:

   | Answers | Recommendation | Alternative |
   | --- | --- | --- |
   | goal = gift | Story | Family book |
   | goal = family | Family book | Story |
   | goal = learn, and age ≤ 3 or doesn't hold a pen yet | Journey stage 1 | Story |
   | goal = learn, age 4 | Journey stage 2 | Dawseyeh KG1 |
   | goal = learn, age 5–7 | Dawseyeh KG2 | Journey stage 3 |

4. **Cart:**
   - multi-item, add-on lines per item, edit/remove
   - "second book −15%" applied to the cheaper item (admin rule)
   - coupon and gift-card code field
   - gift toggle with a 200-char card message and hidden prices on the packing slip
   - cross-sell that reuses the child's existing character
   - Shipping is calculated at the address step.
5. **Payment stays Cash on Delivery only.** Notifications stay email only (ADDENDUM-08 update).
6. **Reuse the character:** once a child has an approved character, every product (stories, workbooks, family book, free cover) reuses it with no new AI cost. Show "شخصية ليان جاهزة" style upsells only when a character exists.
